About this dashboard

ry-intel is my personal spin on a threat intelligence dashboard! I first learned about threat intel at a surface level in one of my cybersecurity classes, took a deeper dive into what actually goes into these dashboards, and was ultimately inspired to build one myself. I was heavily inspired by the look of early 2000s computers and incorporated that in the icons and the expandable 'window' tabs. However, under the retro shell, it tracks CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, the US government's official list of CVEs that attackers are exploiting in the wild right now! Federal agencies are required to fix every entry by its due date under Binding Operational Directive 22-01.

Everything runs client side in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript! It ships with an embedded snapshot of the full catalog so it works anywhere, even offline, and the Refresh button pulls the live CISA feed whenever the hosting allows it. Some of my favorite parts are the desktop icons on the side, the windows that minimize, and the boot screen!

Built by Ryan Dela Cruz. Data source: CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog (public domain).
